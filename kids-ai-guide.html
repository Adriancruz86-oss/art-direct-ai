Hello, Creator

You probably already know AI can answer questions. It can explain things. It can write things. It can make pictures.

But that is not the interesting part.

The interesting part is what you can make with it.

This guide is not about using AI to finish homework. It is not about asking AI to think for you. It is about using a new tool to make something that did not exist before.

A ridiculous invention. A new character. A comic. A game. A gift. Maybe even a book.

First: AI is a tool.

AI cannot see the picture inside your head. The better you explain what matters, the more useful the tool becomes. And sometimes it still gets weird. That is where you come in.

5Make Something for Someone Else

Instead of asking, “What can AI make for me?” ask, “What can I make for somebody?”

Can AI Help You Make a Book?

Jordan eventually asked the bigger question. If AI can help with ideas, words, and pictures, could it make a whole book?

Generate ideas. Delete ideas. Change characters. Fix pictures. Check facts. Rewrite jokes. Ask someone to read it. Change it again. Decide when it is finished.

“Can AI make something?”

For grown-ups

The Point Is Not More Screen Time

Children are going to encounter AI. The goal is not to convince them that AI should do their thinking for them. It is almost the opposite.

Used passively, AI can become an answer machine. Used creatively, it can become a sketchbook, brainstorming partner, prototyping tool, writing assistant, art tool, and starting point.

Encourage kids to question outputs. Ask why they kept one idea and rejected another. Let them catch mistakes. Let them change the prompt. Let them decide when something is funny, useful, strange, or finished.

The best question may not be “What can AI do for my child?”
It may be: “What can my child learn to create with AI?”

Transparency

Yes, AI Helped Make This Guide

AI was used during the creation of this guide. It helped brainstorm, revise words, and create artwork. Sometimes it produced things that were strange, wrong, boring, or unusable.

A human chose what stayed. A human checked the work. A human changed the ideas. A human directed the final guide.

That is not something we are hiding. It is part of the point.

AI is a powerful creative tool.
Tools do not decide what deserves to exist.
People do.
