## Visual assembly rules
- Use existing approved/repaired assets wherever possible.
- Do not regenerate full spreads for layout.
- Real typography throughout.
- Flat page construction; no fake physical-book seams.
- One canonical boy/dog design across all recurring examples.
- Preserve the warm off-white paper and restrained graphite/colored-pencil/light-wash visual language.
- Dense instructional pages must alternate with quieter visual pages.
- No page should look like a software dashboard.
- Checklists may be structured, but should still feel like an illustrator's working sheet.
