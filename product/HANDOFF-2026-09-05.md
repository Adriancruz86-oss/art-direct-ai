## Locked visual direction
For all “good” illustrations:
- loose traditional-paper feel
- graphite + colored pencil + light watercolor/dry gouache
- visible warm off-white paper grain
- minimum pencil strokes, maximum effect
- simplified hair masses
- sparse facial features
- plain clothing unless detail is story-required
- broad/simple backgrounds
- leave areas unfinished / raw paper
- no cinematic lighting, glossy eyes, decorative glow, or overworked skies
- white space is a deliberate design tool

Important production note:
Avoid walking/running poses unless movement truly matters. Clean silhouettes reduce anatomy risk. If a pose adds anatomy risk without storytelling value, simplify it.
