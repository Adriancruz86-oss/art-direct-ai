## CRITICAL REBUILD RULE
Do NOT crop a finished spread into little pieces and paste them into a new page.

We have repeatedly failed by doing this.

If a spread was generated as a complete spread and works as a complete spread:

- keep it intact;
- add a separate editorial page before/after it;
- OR rebuild the WHOLE spread deliberately;
- never "repair" it by chopping half-pages, slicing panels, or harvesting random fragments unless the source was explicitly designed as separable assets.

No visible crop marks.
No white seams.
No page-edge slivers.
No old render text sticking out behind new text boxes.
No pasted-on look.

If rebuilding typography, rebuild the WHOLE spread cleanly.

## CURRENT VISUAL STYLE LOCK
Good artwork must remain consistent with the established book:

- warm off-white paper
- visible paper grain
- graphite / colored pencil
- very light watercolor or dry gouache
- minimum pencil strokes, maximum effect
- simplified hair masses
- sparse facial features
- plain clothing unless detail matters to the story
- broad/simple backgrounds
- intentional unfinished areas
- substantial white space
- imperfect hand-drawn edges

Avoid:

- polished digital illustration
- cinematic lighting
- glossy eyes
- decorative glow
- elaborate skies
- unnecessary foliage
- highly rendered fabric
- individual hair strands
- crisp/over-rendered pets
- generic "AI children's illustration" prettiness

Do not introduce a new character style during rebuilds.

## IMPORTANT LESSON FROM THE BUILD
A new section has been added:

`product/NEW-SECTION-DONT-FIGHT-THE-AI.md`

This section includes the phrase:

**"Drunk off context."**

Meaning:
when a chat accumulates too many failed attempts, corrections, abandoned directions, and old constraints, it may start reusing the wrong pattern even after correctly explaining the latest instruction.

Best-practice lesson for the book:

- stop arguing with the AI;
- ask the current chat for a handoff prompt;
- include exact asset locations;
- move to a fresh chat;
- restate only the CURRENT locked constraints;
- start from the last known-good asset.

This current handoff is itself an example of that workflow.
