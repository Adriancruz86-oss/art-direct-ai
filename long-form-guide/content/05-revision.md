# Part V: Revise in Controlled Passes

Revision becomes unreliable when the instruction is simply "make this better." Better in what way? A cleaner sentence cannot repair a missing premise. A warmer tone cannot verify a quotation. A shorter paragraph cannot fix a chapter in the wrong position.

Revise one dimension at a time. Each pass has a question, evidence, and completion test.

## Pass 1: Promise and structure pass

Question: Does the manuscript deliver the book promise in a coherent sequence?

Review chapter summaries before individual paragraphs. Mark chapters that repeat a function, depend on missing material, arrive too early, or do not change the reader or story.

Completion test:

- Every part has a clear movement.
- Every chapter has a distinct primary job.
- Dependencies appear before use.
- The middle increases difficulty or pressure.
- The ending completes the promise.

## Pass 2: Chapter-purpose and sequence pass

Compare each chapter to its approved brief. Identify sections that migrated from another chapter, openings that restart the whole book, and endings that merely summarize.

Create a one-sentence handoff between adjacent chapters: "Because Chapter 8 established X, Chapter 9 can now ask Y." If the sentence cannot be written, inspect the transition or order.

## Pass 3: Continuity or argument pass

[FICTION]
Check character knowledge, motivation, injuries, objects, travel, time, setting rules, emotional states, setup, and payoff. Read each major character's scenes as a separate sequence.

[NONFICTION]
Check definitions, claims, examples, objections, practices, and cross-references. Confirm that recurring terms keep the same meaning or that changes are explicitly developed.

## Pass 4: Evidence and citation pass

Return to every claim-and-source record. Open the source. Confirm that it supports the sentence as written. Verify names, dates, numbers, quotation marks, page locations, and bibliography details.

AI cannot perform the final evidence and citation pass by inspecting its own output. It can help create a claim inventory, but a human must return to the sources.

[BIBLICAL PROJECT]
Perform a theological consistency review and a Scripture-in-context review. Check every reference against the chosen translation policy. Confirm the manuscript distinguishes observation, interpretation, theological conclusion, and application. Ask a qualified human reviewer to examine the treatment of disputed passages and sensitive doctrine.

## Pass 5: Voice and audience pass

Read for relationship, not just style. Is the author speaking with the reader, at the reader, or past the reader?

For the biblical project, perform a youth-audience suitability review:

- Is the language understandable without becoming childish?
- Do examples acknowledge different stages from 13 through 21?
- Does challenge preserve dignity?
- Does the book invite honest help-seeking?
- Are family and church situations treated with appropriate complexity?
- Could any instruction isolate a reader who is unsafe?

Read representative pages aloud. Mark sentences the author would never naturally say.

## Pass 6: Repetition and compression pass

AI drafts commonly repeat at four levels:

- The same claim in different chapters
- The same explanation within a chapter
- The same sentence structure within a page
- The same conclusion at the end of every section

Build a list of key claims by chapter. Decide whether each repetition deepens, applies, recalls, or merely restates. Cut or combine accidental repetition.

Compression is not making every sentence short. It is removing words that do not change meaning, rhythm, emphasis, or relationship.

## Pass 7: AI-tell pass

Search for patterns, not forbidden words:

- Generic scene-setting such as "in today's fast-paced world"
- Repeated three-item lists
- Constant contrast formulas: "not X, but Y"
- Inflated transitions and conclusions
- Equal paragraph lengths and overly symmetrical sections
- Abstract encouragement after every difficult point
- Excessive explanation of familiar ideas
- False balance between positions with different evidence
- Unearned certainty

Do not mechanically delete every pattern. Decide whether it reflects the author's intention or the model's default rhythm.

## Pass 8: Sentence-level polish

Only now revise sentences for clarity, rhythm, precision, and emphasis.

Prefer concrete subjects and verbs. Replace vague references. Vary sentence length intentionally. Confirm pronouns and antecedents. Remove metaphors that compete. Preserve useful roughness when it carries voice.

Give an AI assistant short passages and explicit constraints. Require it to explain proposed changes or return a comparison table. Never replace the manuscript wholesale at this stage.

## Pass 9: Read-aloud and final human review

Read the manuscript aloud or listen to a careful text-to-speech pass while following the text. Mark breath problems, hidden repetition, tone shifts, and sentences that are technically correct but emotionally false.

Use several human reviewers with defined roles:

- Developmental reader for structure and promise
- Subject expert for factual or professional accuracy
- Theological reviewer for biblical interpretation and consistency
- Youth-informed reader for age, tone, and safety
- Copyeditor or proofreader for language and consistency

One reader may fill more than one role, but the roles should not disappear.

## Revision requests that work

Weak: "Make this more engaging."

Stronger:

> Diagnose this section only for distance from the reader. Identify abstract claims that lack a concrete situation, moments where the voice becomes parental or shaming, and paragraphs that repeat rather than advance. Do not rewrite. Return location, diagnosis, reader effect, and two revision options.

The author reviews the diagnosis before requesting prose.

## Revision gate

The manuscript advances to assembly only when:

- All nine passes are complete.
- Open structural problems are resolved.
- Claims and quotations are verified.
- Continuity trackers agree with the manuscript.
- Sensitive guidance has qualified review.
- No unresolved issue is hidden inside a comment or chat.
