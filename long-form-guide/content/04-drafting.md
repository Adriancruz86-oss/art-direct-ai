# Part IV: Draft Chapter by Chapter

The drafting unit is not the book. It is not even always the chapter. The safest unit is a purposeful section small enough to evaluate against an approved brief.

This part describes the drafting loop used for every chapter.

## The drafting loop

1. Review the project controls.
2. Resolve blocking author decisions.
3. Divide the chapter into beats, scenes, or argument moves.
4. Generate options before prose when useful.
5. Draft one controlled section.
6. Review the section against the brief and evidence.
7. Approve, revise, or reject it.
8. Update trackers and create the chapter handoff.

## 1. Review before generating

Open the project bible, outline entry, chapter brief, voice sheet, and relevant trackers. Do not rely on what you remember from the last session.

Ask:

- What is this chapter's one primary job?
- What must change for the reader or character?
- Which material is required?
- Which material is excluded?
- What must remain consistent?
- What decision belongs to the author before drafting begins?

If the chapter brief is vague, repair it. Generating prose will not clarify an unresolved purpose.

## 2. Resolve blocking decisions

Examples of blocking decisions:

- Which interpretation or evidence standard will the author use?
- Which character makes the irreversible choice?
- Is the chapter primarily explanation or application?
- Does this scene reveal the secret or only increase suspicion?
- Is the reader being challenged, reassured, or equipped?

Ask the assistant for contrasting options with consequences. Then decide and update the log.

Do not ask the model to choose a theological conclusion, moral stance, final plot meaning, or personal testimony for you.

## 3. Build the internal map

A nonfiction chapter can be divided into argument moves:

1. Opening situation or question
2. Misconception
3. Source or evidence in context
4. Principle
5. Example
6. Objection or complication
7. Application
8. Reflection and transition

A fiction chapter can be divided into scenes or scene beats:

1. Immediate goal
2. Obstacle
3. Escalation
4. Choice
5. Consequence
6. New story state

The internal map prevents the assistant from spending half the word budget on an attractive opening and rushing the chapter's actual work.

## 4. Generate options before prose

Use AI first where its abundance is useful.

Ask for:

- Three opening situations with different emotional effects
- Several examples that test the same principle
- Competing objections ranked by seriousness
- Scene complications that arise from established character motives
- Analogies with stated limits
- Alternative transitions

Require brief options, not polished pages. Compare them against the chapter brief. Combine, reject, or rewrite.

[WARNING]
Option generation is not source generation. A plausible statistic or quotation is not an option to use. It is an unverified lead.

## 5. Draft one section

Give the assistant:

- Relevant context-reset packet
- Approved internal map
- Exact section to draft
- Word range
- Required source or scene facts
- Voice constraints
- Prohibitions
- Output format

Example instruction:

> Draft only the 450-600-word section that distinguishes restraint from passivity. Use the approved online-retaliation situation. Preserve the defined meaning of strength. Do not quote Scripture, introduce a new theological claim, diagnose anger, or conclude the chapter. End by creating the question the next section answers.

The boundaries make review possible.

## 6. Review the section

Review in this order:

### Function

Does the section do its assigned job? Remove material that belongs to another section, even if it is good.

### Truth and continuity

Verify claims, quotations, sources, character facts, timeline, and terminology. Verify every quotation outside the AI assistant. For Scripture, open the selected translation and confirm the wording and reference.

### Reasoning

Does the conclusion follow? Has the draft skipped a difficult step? Does an example prove less than the prose claims?

### Voice

Does it sound like this author and this book? Identify generic transitions, symmetrical lists, inflated conclusions, and repeated sentence patterns.

### Movement

Does the ending create the next needed question or story state?

Only after those checks should you polish individual sentences.

## 7. Approve, revise, or reject

Use three clear outcomes:

- **Approve:** The section fulfills its function and can enter the working manuscript.
- **Revise:** The underlying move is sound, but a defined problem must be corrected.
- **Reject:** The section solves the wrong problem, relies on false material, breaks the architecture, or has no recoverable voice.

Do not spend six revisions rescuing a draft that misunderstood the job. Return to the internal map and begin again.

## 8. Update the system

After approval:

- Add verified claims and sources to the ledger.
- Add new definitions and callbacks to continuity.
- Record decisions that changed during drafting.
- Log problems reserved for later revision passes.
- Update word count against budget.
- At chapter completion, create the chapter handoff.

The operating system stays current because updates happen when decisions are fresh.

## When to start a clean conversation

Start a clean conversation when:

- The assistant repeatedly follows an old instruction that is no longer valid.
- The conversation contains several rejected directions.
- The model confuses chapters, characters, or versions.
- A new phase requires a different role, such as moving from brainstorming to verification.
- You spend more effort correcting context than supplying it.
- The current task can be described more clearly with a fresh context-reset packet.

Do not treat one chat as the book. Your external documents are the book's memory.

## Fiction drafting controls

[FICTION]
For every scene, track:

- Viewpoint character
- Scene goal
- Opposition
- Information available to each character
- Emotional starting state
- Choice or reversal
- Physical and temporal continuity
- Ending state
- Setup or payoff affected

Ask AI for scene options grounded in established motives. Reject conflict that requires a character to become temporarily foolish. Reject surprises that violate world rules or appear without setup.

After a scene, update character knowledge. A later chapter cannot conceal information the viewpoint character already learned.

## Nonfiction drafting controls

[NONFICTION]
For every argument move, track:

- Claim
- Evidence
- Source quality
- Explanation of how the evidence supports the claim
- Limitation
- Reasonable objection
- Application

Separate illustrative stories from evidence. A personal account can make a principle memorable without proving that the principle is universally true.

Do not let confident prose widen a narrow source. If a study concerns one population, the manuscript cannot silently apply it to everyone.

## Biblical-project drafting controls

[BIBLICAL PROJECT]
Use this order when a section depends on Scripture:

1. Identify the passage and translation policy.
2. Read and record the immediate context.
3. Record observations about the text.
4. State the interpretation the author is adopting.
5. Identify the theological framework when the conclusion depends on it.
6. Draw the principle with appropriate limits.
7. Create a modern application.
8. Review the application for age, safety, and unintended harm.

Verify every quotation and reference manually. Never ask a model to "quote the verse from memory" and publish the result.

### Writing across ages 13-21

Write the main prose to the chosen midpoint. Vary examples rather than switching voices.

For a chapter on work:

- A younger example may involve chores completed without supervision.
- A middle example may involve a first job and admitting a mistake.
- An older example may involve money, schedule, or responsibility to coworkers.

The principle remains stable while responsibility expands.

### Challenge without shame

Challenge names responsibility and a next action. Shame turns struggle into identity and often makes honesty less safe.

Prefer:

> Courage may begin with telling a trusted adult what has been happening.

Avoid:

> A real man handles his own problems.

The second sentence can isolate a reader facing danger, abuse, addiction, or mental-health distress. Qualified human review is required for sensitive guidance.

### Application without overreach

Application should answer:

- What can the reader notice?
- What decision can the reader make?
- What action is safe and appropriate?
- Who can help?
- What outcome is not promised?

The book can call a reader toward faithfulness. It cannot guarantee a particular response from family, friends, employers, romantic interests, or God.

## Managing word count during drafting

Track three numbers:

- Planned words
- Approved words
- Revision variance

If a chapter exceeds its maximum, diagnose before cutting:

- Did the chapter acquire a second job?
- Is the same idea explained more than once?
- Are examples doing identical work?
- Did background research replace the reader's actual need?
- Does a section belong in another chapter or appendix?

If a chapter is too short, do not inflate it. Check for missing evidence, missing complication, insufficient application, or an unresolved dependency.

## A complete chapter session

A disciplined session can follow this sequence:

1. Read the chapter brief and current handoff.
2. Select one internal move.
3. Load only relevant context.
4. Resolve one author decision.
5. Generate brief options if needed.
6. Choose and adapt the option.
7. Draft the section.
8. Review function, truth, reasoning, voice, and movement.
9. Approve, revise, or reject.
10. Update ledgers and trackers.
11. Record the next exact task.

Stopping with a clear next task is more valuable than exhausting yourself into three additional pages of uncontrolled prose.

## Chapter handoff checklist

At chapter completion, record:

- Final chapter job and word count
- Reader or character movement
- Claims and sources introduced
- Definitions established
- Fiction facts or nonfiction promises created
- Questions deferred
- Revision issues logged
- Next chapter dependency
- One-paragraph summary

The chapter handoff lets the next chapter begin from reality rather than memory.
