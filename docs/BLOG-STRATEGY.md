# Art Direct AI Blog Strategy

## Positioning
**Make AI work look directed, not generated.**

Art Direct AI is a practical creative-direction publication for people using AI to make books, illustrations, covers, writing, and digital products.

The public promise is not “hide the AI.” The promise is to make deliberate creative decisions instead of accepting model defaults.

## Audience
- Indie authors and publishers
- Illustrators adapting AI into their process
- Creators building digital products
- Small creative businesses using generative tools

## Core content pillars
1. **Creative Tasking** — break complex creative jobs into decisions, constraints, passes, and corrections.
2. **Art Direction** — composition, style language, continuity, restraint, visual hierarchy.
3. **The Human Pass** — detect and remove generic phrasing, visual clichés, repetition, over-polish, and other AI defaults.
4. **Workflows** — real build/failure/fix case studies that produce repeatable systems.

## Editorial engine
**Build → Break → Fix → Teach → Product**

The work creates the content. Articles should come from real creative problems rather than invented SEO filler.

## House concepts
- **Default Output Problem:** the model gravitates toward statistically safe, familiar choices.
- **Creative Tasking:** assign a focused creative job with constraints and decision criteria.
- **Human Pass:** deliberate revision where taste and judgment replace model defaults.
- **Prompt Drift:** repeated revisions slowly move away from the intended target.
- **Visual Anchors:** recurring traits that preserve identity and continuity.
- **One-Shot Syndrome:** expecting a single large prompt to produce finished creative work.

## Commercial rule
Every article may point to one primary product, but the article must still solve a useful problem on its own.

The CTA sells depth and repeatability—not withheld basic information.
